💡 What I Built: Autonomous AI Creator

Every day, thousands of AI posts flood LinkedIn and X—but almost all of them exist because a human wrote the first prompt.

For ViCoDathon 2026, I challenged myself to break prompt dependency and build Autonomous AI Creator: an AI technology persona that no longer waits for human instructions. Once initialized, it researches, evaluates, writes, remembers, and publishes technical intelligence 24/7 over time.

---

🛠️ How It Works Under the Hood:

1. Live Web Discovery Engine 🌐
   Scans real-time tech breakthroughs from Tavily API, Google News RSS, and Hacker News without human prompts.

2. Editorial Quality Judgment (Google Gemini 1.5 Flash) ⚖️
   Acts as a critical Managing Editor—evaluating candidates on novelty, depth, and relevance, rejecting fluff, and only publishing topics scoring ≥ 7.5 / 10.

3. Cognitive Memory & Deduplication (Breeth AI + Postgres) 🧠
   Combines PostgreSQL Trigram Similarity (pg_trgm) and Breeth AI Memory Graphs to remember past posts, preventing repetition and maintaining historical continuity across publishing cycles.

4. Transparent Editorial Rationale 💡
   Every API response (GET /api/agent/feed) includes the post text alongside explicit publishing rationale—explaining why the topic was selected, why it is urgent, and citing exact source links.

5. 24/7 Deployment Stack (Vercel + Render + Neon Postgres) ⚡
   Deployed across Vercel (Frontend Studio UI), Render (Node.js/Express Backend API), and Neon (PostgreSQL Database). Complemented by a high-tech Dark Glassmorphic UI featuring live telemetry logs and persona switching (Ada for AI Security, Marcus for ML Systems, Elena for Governance, Devon for Open Source).

---

📡 Evaluator API Endpoints:
- POST /api/agent/init — Initialized exactly once to start the autonomous loop.
- GET /api/agent/feed?agentId=abc-123 — Polled periodically by evaluators to observe new posts appearing over time.

Built using Google Antigravity, Google Gemini, ChatGPT, and GitHub Copilot during an unforgettable vibe-coding sprint.

This experience pushed my limits as a developer, and seeing the system operating live across Vercel, Render, and Neon feels tremendous!
